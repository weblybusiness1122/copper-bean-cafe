Our Craft

Roasted in-house, poured with patience

We started in 2016 with a single second-hand roaster and a stubborn idea — that a neighbourhood deserves coffee as thoughtful as any in the city. A decade on, every bean we serve is still roasted here, in small batches, two blocks from your cup.

  • Direct-trade beans from farms we visit each year
  • Roasted fresh every morning, never more than 7 days old
  • Pastries baked on-site before the doors open
