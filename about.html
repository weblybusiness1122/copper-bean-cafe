Since 2016

It began with one borrowed roaster

The Copper Bean started the way most good things do — a little recklessly. In the winter of 2016, two friends signed a lease on a draughty corner shop on Marlowe Street, dragged in a second-hand drum roaster, and spent three months learning, batch by scorched batch, how to coax sweetness out of a green bean.

What we lacked in experience we made up for in stubbornness. We wanted a place that felt like the best parts of a good kitchen and a quiet library at once — somewhere you could land for five minutes or five hours, where the coffee was serious but nobody took themselves too seriously.

A decade later the roaster is newer and the room is warmer, but the idea hasn't moved an inch. Every bean we serve is still roasted here in small batches. Every pastry is still shaped by hand before sunrise. And we still, genuinely, want to know how your day is going.

The Ambience

Warm light, worn oak, no hurry

Step inside and the city goes quiet. Morning sun spills across reclaimed-oak tables and the slow hiss of the espresso machine sets the rhythm. Soft jazz, the smell of fresh lamination from the bakery, well-worn leather and plants that have outgrown their pots. We designed every corner to be lingered in — a window bench for the dreamers, a long communal table for the workers, and deep armchairs by the fire for the readers.

What We Stand For

Three things we never cut corners on

Roasted in-house

Our drum roaster runs most mornings. Beans go from roaster to grinder in days, not months, so every cup tastes the way the farmer intended — alive, sweet and never flat.

Direct, fair trade

We buy green coffee straight from farms we visit each harvest — in Ethiopia, Colombia and Guatemala. Fair prices, long relationships, full traceability on every bag.

People first

Baristas on a living wage, regulars we greet by name, and a counter that's always had a free babyccino for the little ones. Hospitality is the whole point.

Worth Trying

The Pour

Ethiopia Yirgacheffe

Our barista's desert-island brew. Floral, tea-like, with jasmine and bergamot that open up as it cools. Hand-poured on the V60.

The Signature

Copper Maple Latte

The drink that quietly became our name in the neighbourhood — smoked maple and cinnamon bark folded into a double-shot latte.

The Bake

Cardamom Bun

Our baker's Scandinavian roots on a plate — twisted, spiced and crowned with pearl sugar. Best eaten warm, torn by hand.

The Journey

How we got here

’16

The draughty corner shop

We open on Marlowe Street with one second-hand roaster, twelve seats and a hand-written menu taped to the window.

’18

Our first harvest trip

We fly to Yirgacheffe and shake hands with the family who still grows our flagship single-origin today.

’20

The bakery is born

We knock through the back wall and build a bakery, laminating our first croissants at 4am. The queue starts the next week.

’23

A bigger roaster

Demand outgrows the little drum. We install a new 15kg roaster and begin supplying beans to kitchens across the city.

’26

Ten years, same table

A decade in, still independent, still on Marlowe Street, still roasting every bean we pour.
